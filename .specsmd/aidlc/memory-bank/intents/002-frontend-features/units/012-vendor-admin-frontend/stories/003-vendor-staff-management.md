# Story: Vendor Staff Management

## Story ID
`003-vendor-staff-management`

## Unit
`012-vendor-admin-frontend`

## Requirement Mapping
- **FR-FE-7**: Vendor staff onboarding, role assignment (Vendor Operator / Staff), permission management, and staff activity overview.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Vendor Admin can view a list of vendor staff members with status (Active, Pending, Suspended) and assigned roles.
2. Vendor Admin can invite new staff members by email and assign standard staff permissions.
3. Vendor Admin can revoke or update staff role access.
4. Activity audit log shows recent staff actions within the vendor tenant space.
